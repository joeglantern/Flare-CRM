What a plan switches on

Some things are in every plan because a CRM without them is not a CRM: signing in, two-factor, users and teams, contacts, companies, notes and files, tasks, the timeline, notifications, search and the manual. The rest is a switch. Switching a feature off hides it and refuses its routes. It never deletes anything, so switching it back on restores what was there.

What a plan limits

Five numbers. Reaching one does not break anything: the request that would go over it is refused with a message saying which limit and by how much, and freeing a seat makes the same request work.

Active usersA hard cap on creating or reactivating an account.
StorageAttachments, recordings and backups together, in GB.
Recording retentionA ceiling in days on the setting you choose.
ChannelsHow many WhatsApp or SMS numbers are connected.
PipelinesHow many deal pipelines you can run.

If an account lapses

Everything stays readable and exportable. Nothing is deleted. Writing stops, and a banner names who to call. Incoming WhatsApp messages and web form submissions keep landing so nothing is lost while it is sorted out. The plan is a document signed by us and checked on your own server, so this holds whether or not your server can reach the internet. There is no kill switch, and there should not be one.
