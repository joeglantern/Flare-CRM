01

Your own server, your own address

Each company gets its own installation and its own database at yourcompany.flarehub.co.ke, or your own domain. Nobody else's data is in the same table.

02

An audit log nobody can edit

Every consequential action, including refusals and our own support actions, is written to a log that no administrator can change or delete.

03

Backups and retention you set

Nightly backups, the last fourteen kept. Recordings and records are deleted on the schedule you choose, by a job that runs whether anyone remembers or not.

If an account lapses

Everything stays readable and exportable. Nothing is deleted. Writing stops, and a banner names who to call. Incoming WhatsApp messages and web form submissions keep landing so nothing is lost while it is sorted out.

The plan is a document signed by us and checked on your own server, so this holds whether or not your server can reach the internet. There is no kill switch, and there should not be one.
