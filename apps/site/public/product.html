Product

Five things it does, shown at the size you will use them.

Every screenshot on this page is the real product. Figure numbers so you can ask about one on the call.

01

Calls

Connected to your Yeastar PBX. The product knows who is calling before you answer, dials from any number on any screen, and keeps the recording against the call.

  • Screen pop within a third of a second of the ring, on whatever page is open
  • Click to call from any phone number in the product
  • Live calls board for managers: ringing, talking, who and since when
  • Call outcomes you define, set at the end of every call
  • Recordings kept for the number of days you set, with every play audited
02

WhatsApp

One WhatsApp Business number, one inbox, one thread per customer. Unassigned messages sit in a queue until somebody picks them up, and the whole team sees the same history.

  • One conversation per customer, not per agent
  • Assign, close, reopen and archive
  • The 24 hour reply window is shown, and templates take over when it closes
  • Delivery ticks: queued, sent, delivered, read, failed with the reason
  • Unknown numbers become contacts in one click
03

The timeline

Contacts, companies, leads and deals each have one. Calls, messages, notes, tasks, files and stage changes land on it in order, with a time and a name, whoever did them.

  • Contacts with several phones and emails, a company, an owner and tags
  • Leads that convert to a contact, a company and a deal in one step
  • Notes with mentions, files against any record
  • Custom fields of nine types on every entity
  • Duplicates found by phone and email, merged with the history kept
04

Deals

A board per pipeline, stages you name with a probability each, and a forecast weighted by them. Won and lost carry a reason so the conversion report means something.

  • Drag between stages, totals per column
  • Value in shillings, expected close, owner, the next task
  • Stage history on every deal
  • Forecast by close month, weighted and unweighted
05

Reports

Six reports, each a table you can export and a chart you can read. Agents see their own numbers, managers their team, administrators everything.

  • Calls summary, agent performance, missed calls
  • Pipeline summary, conversion, forecast
  • Every report exports to CSV and prints cleanly
  • Times in Africa/Nairobi, not UTC

Also in the box, because a CRM without them is not one.

Two-factor authentication Roles: admin, manager, agent Teams and ownership Global search and a command palette Tasks with a calendar CSV import with column mapping Web forms that create leads A manual with real screenshots
