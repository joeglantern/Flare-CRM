Your installation

Your own server, your own database, your own address at yourcompany.flarehub.co.ke. Administrators get their welcome email and set their own password. Nobody sets it for them.

1 day

Your own domain

If you want crm.yourcompany.co.ke, you publish two DNS records and we verify both. The certificate is automatic.

Most of the day is DNS propagating. Skip this step and you are live the same day.

3
Half a day

Phone system

Your Yeastar PBX is connected and each person is mapped to their extension. Usually remote; on site if your network needs it.

Needs the Yeastar P-Series Enterprise or Ultimate plan, because the API is not in the others. We check this on the discovery call.

Leave it for later and it does not hold up going live.
